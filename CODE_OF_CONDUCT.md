# Code of Conduct

tradforge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participating in tradforge to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect the work of translators: this project exists to help them, not to replace their judgement.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@ScottMichel](https://github.com/ScottMichel),
privately on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors temporarily or
permanently.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including its enforcement guidelines, applies.
